# Short Papers

This folder contains the handwritten short-paper sequence for `scope_wr121`.

## Naming convention

- Assignment ID: `U1L<week>SP`
- Folder: `papers/U1L<week>SP/`
- Student files: `assignment.tex` and `assignment.pdf`

`SP` means **Short Paper**. The lesson number identifies the week after which the paper is assigned.

## Sequence

| Assignment ID | Week | Title | Length | Status |
|---|---:|---|---|---|
| `U1L6SP` | 6 | *Prophecy Is Not Proof?* | 1–2 handwritten pages | Built |

## Course rule

These papers are closed-world, handwritten compositions. Students may use the assigned primary text, course handouts, and their own class notes. They may not use AI, the internet, outside criticism or summaries, or assistance from another person.
