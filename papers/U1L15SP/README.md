# U1L15SP — What Makes a Man?

- Placement: after Week 15
- Product: third handwritten short paper
- Format: approximately 2 handwritten pages on separate lined paper
- Score: 12 points
- Sources: *Macbeth*, course handouts, and the student's own class notes only
- Student assignment: `assignment.tex` / `assignment.pdf`
- Textual-evidence supplement: `text-supplement.tex` / `text-supplement.pdf`

The paper asks students to clarify competing meanings of `man` and `manhood`, classify Macbeth and Lady Macbeth's dispute as verbal, real, or mixed, and make a qualified comparative judgment without treating aggression as the teacher-approved definition of masculinity.

The planning page is shorter and more independent than `U1L10SP`: students map the contested term, select evidence, test later consequences, and choose their own organization.

The neutral supplement supplies *Macbeth* 1.5 (Lady Macbeth's invocation) and 1.7 (the manhood dispute), with factual context and vocabulary support. The excerpts are anchors rather than limits; remembered later events may be paraphrased accurately, but quotations may not be invented from memory.

This is not a Krewone answer-box worksheet and has no `template.json` or AI-grading key.
