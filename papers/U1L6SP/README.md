# U1L6SP — Prophecy Is Not Proof?

- Placement: after Week 6
- Product: first low-stakes short paper
- Format: 1–2 handwritten pages on separate lined paper
- Sources: *Macbeth*, course handouts, and the student's own class notes only
- Student packet: `assignment.tex` / `assignment.pdf`

This assignment is intentionally brief and lightly scaffolded. The planning page guides students through claim, argument, form test, fact test, evidence, and qualification without prescribing a conclusion or paragraph formula.

It is not a Krewone answer-box worksheet and has no `template.json` or AI-grading key.
