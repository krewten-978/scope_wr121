# WR121 — 2026–2027 Final Preliminary Calendar

## Course structure

`scope_wr121` is planned as a full-year WR121 writing course organized around Richard Whately's logic, philosophical writing, Shakespeare, formal literature papers, Logic Dungeons, and a final portfolio defense.

The repository's nineteen numbered cycles are instructional cycles, not automatic calendar weeks.

### Normal cycle

- Day 1: Student Reading and Logic Lab
- Day 2: Lit Example reader and Lit Example worksheet
- Day 3: finish Monday/Tuesday work; introduce the informal paper
- Days 4–5: write, revise, and submit the informal paper

### Paper rules

- Informal papers are independent philosophical writing practice, not extensions of the Lit Examples.
- A one-day loss does not cancel the informal paper.
- A two-or-more-day loss cancels the informal paper for that week.
- A Logic Dungeon replaces the informal paper regardless of the number of available days.
- Formal literature papers receive a full writing week unless the calendar requires a short continuation.
- The final portfolio defense counts as a formal paper for Semester 2.
- The teacher's confirmed stopping point overrides this projection.

## Logic Dungeon cadence

- U1F1LD: after Cycles 1–3
- U1F2LD: after Cycles 4–6
- U1F3LD: after Cycles 7–9
- U1F4LD: after Cycles 10–12
- Final cumulative Dungeon: after Cycle 17, before the portfolio workshop

- Thursday–Friday: write and submit the first informal paper.

Informal Paper 1:

Formal Paper B:

> **Does truth exist independently of what people believe?**

This expands the earlier informal topic into a formal argument. Students should distinguish truth from agreement, usefulness, sincerity, confidence, and social power; develop a position; use examples and counterexamples; address an objection; and qualify the conclusion. No formal research is required.

### Reserve weeks

The remaining calendar weeks should remain open for:

- a formal paper that takes longer than one week;
- cycle spillover after shortened weeks;
- reteaching after a Dungeon;
- missing work and make-up assignments;
- portfolio conferences and revision;
- a final course synthesis.

Do not automatically add new required content merely because reserve days remain.
